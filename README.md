# EDA on Retail Sales Data

## Project Overview
In this project, I performed Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on a retail dataset to understand sales trends, customer behavior, and product performance.

## Tools Used
- Python  
- Pandas  
- Matplotlib  
- Seaborn  
- Jupyter Notebook  

## What I Did
- Loaded and checked the dataset  
- Analyzed basic statistics like mean, median, and standard deviation  
- Studied monthly and quarterly sales trends  
- Analyzed customer segments  
- Found top-selling products and category-wise sales  
- Created a correlation heatmap  
- Compared sales and profit  
- Analyzed sales by region  

## Key Insights
- Sales are not the same across all regions  
- Technology category has the highest sales  
- Few products contribute most of the revenue  
- High sales do not always mean high profit  

## What I Learned
- How to clean and understand real-world data  
- How to use visualization to find patterns  
- Importance of analyzing profit, not just sales  

## Conclusion
From this analysis, I understood that business performance depends on region, product demand, and proper profit management.

## Recommendations
- Focus more on high-performing regions  
- Keep top-selling products in stock  
- Improve pricing strategy to avoid losses
